Command Summary
The mathematical constant
π
Command Syntax
π
Menu Location
Press 2nd π to paste the π symbol.
Calculator Compatibility
TI-83/84/+/SE
Token Size
1 byte
The π constant on the calculator approximates the mathematical constant
π
(pi), defined as the ratio of a circle's circumference to its diameter. It's fairly important in many areas of mathematics, but especially trigonometry. Probably the most common use of π on the TI-83 series calculators is for dealing with angles in
radian
form.
The approximate value of π stored on the calculator is 3.1415926535898.
