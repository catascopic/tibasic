Command Summary

i

Command Syntax

i
Menu Location

Calculator Compatibility

Token Size
1 byte
The
a+b
i
command puts the calculator into rectangular complex number mode. This means that:
Taking square roots of negative numbers, and similar operations, no longer returns an error.
Complex results are displayed in the form
a+b
i
(hence the name of the command)
This is the standard way of displaying complex numbers, though they can also be displayed in polar form (see
re^θi
for more details). To extract the coefficients a and b, use the
real(
and
imag(
commands.
Advanced Uses
Rather than switch to a+b
i
mode, you might want to force the calculations to use complex numbers by making the original argument complex. The general way to do this is by adding +0
i
