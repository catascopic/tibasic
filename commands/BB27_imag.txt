imag(z)
returns the imaginary part of the complex number
z
. If
z
is represented as
x
+i
y
where
x
and
y
are both real,
imag(z)
returns
y
. Also works on a list of complex numbers.
imag(3+4i)
     4

imag({3+4i,-2i,17})
